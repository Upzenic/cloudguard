Questions

Frequently asked

Is it safe to connect my AWS account? +
Yes. We use read-only access — we can see your billing data but cannot create, modify, or delete anything in your AWS account. The same access level your accountant would have. Hundreds of startups have connected safely.
How long does setup take? +
Most customers are set up in under 10 minutes. We send you a step-by-step guide — no technical knowledge needed. If you get stuck, WhatsApp us and we'll walk you through it live.
Do I need to be technical to use this? +
Not at all. CloudGuard is built specifically for founders and finance teams who are not AWS experts. Everything is explained in plain language — no jargon, no confusing graphs.
What if I want to cancel? +
Cancel any time from your account settings — no forms, no calls, no questions. Your data is deleted within 7 days of cancellation. We make it easy because we want you to stay because the product works, not because leaving is hard.
Do you support GCP or Azure too? +
Right now we support AWS only. GCP support is coming in early 2025 and Azure later that year. If you'd like to be notified, drop your email and we'll let you know first.
